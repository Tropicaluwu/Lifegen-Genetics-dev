<strong>LifeGen Genetics v1.1: the cat maker update</strong>

The custom cat maker (making your own main character) is now fully genetic:
- Pressing Customize with a cat picked starts from that cat (its exact genes, sex, fur, poses, scars and accessories)
- Genemod's real colors, patterns, point types and eye colors, with heterochromia as the second eye and sectoral eyes
- One white list: the white genes plus every white patch (Genemod's, ClanGen's and color spots), pick as many as you like, in groups with "clear all" and "picked patches". The white genes follow how much white you pick, so kits get about that much
- Torties: pick the patches, and a patch color or pattern of their own (which makes the cat a chimera, with a note saying so). Unticking and ticking Tortie again keeps them
- Fur genes (rex coats, sphynx, LaPerm, lykoi, brush coat, satin, glitter) and body genes (ears, tails, legs, body type, size) on the last page
- More options for each list (sunshine, silver, wideband, bleaching, ghosting, pale markings...), scars you can check and uncheck, and lists that stay where you scrolled
- The cat maker starts from a plain cat, and your cat comes out exactly like the preview (including the sex you picked)

Names:
- A Names button on the main menu (and in the Clan settings) to add and take out names: prefixes, suffixes, rank endings (-kit, -paw, -star), loner names, kittypet names, Clan names and blocked names
- Changes can be for all saves or for just one save, and they stack (a save uses the all-saves changes plus its own)
- Any list can use only your names, and you can pick how often cats get your prefixes and suffixes (since most cats are named after their looks). Search, pages and "reset list" make big lists easy

<strong>LifeGen Genetics v1.0</strong>

LifeGen and Genemod (the genetics mod) put together into one game.

All credit goes to the LifeGen team (github.com/sedgestripe/clangen) and to Chinch-Bug and the Genemod contributors (github.com/Chinch-Bug/clangen-genemod), and to the ClanGen team they're both built on. This is just the two of them combined.

Built on the latest stable versions: ClanGen v0.13.4, LifeGen v0.7.7.5 and Genemod 0.13.

Genetics (from Genemod):
- Every cat has real genes. Kits inherit coat color, patterns, white, eyes, fur length, ear and tail traits and more from their parents
- Genemod's appearance descriptions, genotype tooltips and offspring prediction
- Genemod's new names, suffixes and prefixes (can be turned off in the clan settings)
- Genetic conditions like deafness in blue eyed white cats, manx syndrome, folds and so on
- Optional multi clan mode when making a new clan, where the other clans have their own real cats
- Healers, queens and queen apprentices use Genemod's ranks
- Favorites have 6 colors (right click the star to remove it)

LifeGen saves:
- Normal LifeGen saves load in! Cats get genes made from their old pelts the first time the save is loaded
- "Accurate Porting" is on by default so cats keep their look instead of getting new genes from their parents. You can turn it off in the settings if you want genetics to take over
- Once a save is played here it can't go back into regular LifeGen
- Saves go in their own folder so they don't mix with LifeGen's

Making your cat:
- Pick one of the random cats, or press customize to use the LifeGen customizer. Whatever you pick in the customizer gets turned into genes

Kits:
- With "same sex birth" on, either parent can carry the kits, whatever their gender. The kits are born in the Clan of whoever carries them, and that cat gets the pregnant and recovering conditions (before, the kits could be born in the other parent's Clan)

ClanGen v0.13.4:
- ClanGen v0.13.4's bug fixes, typo fixes and new events
- A few new Gathering events about the other Clans and their new leaders
- Healers that join from loner patrols get the wandering healer backstory, and random loners and rogues don't get it anymore
- Herb patrols have a cap on how many herbs each cat can carry, clever cats can carry more

Speed:
- Moons are faster in big Clans: ranks aren't worked out from scratch millions of times a moon, cat lists check the Clan first, and a trait file isn't read from the disk thousands of times a moon
